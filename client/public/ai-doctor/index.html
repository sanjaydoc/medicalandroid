  • Understand symptoms and when to seek care

  • Read and explain a blood test, ECG, X-ray, MRI, CT or prescription
  • Post-operative and recovery care

What an AI doctor is not: MediMind cannot examine you, and it does not give a diagnosis or a personal prescription. It's a knowledgeable first step to help you understand your health and prepare for a real consultation. For anything needing examination, tests or a prescription — and for emergencies — see a qualified clinician.

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI doctor free?

Yes — free in your browser or the Android app, 24/7, no appointment needed.

Can an AI doctor diagnose me?

No. It gives general, educational information and helps you understand symptoms, medicines and reports. It cannot examine you or give a diagnosis or prescription — see a clinician for that.

What can I ask?

Symptoms and when to seek care, how medicines work, what a lab report or scan shows, recovery care, and general health questions — in any language.

Is it safe?

MediMind is for general information and always points you to a clinician for anything needing examination or a prescription. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number immediately.
