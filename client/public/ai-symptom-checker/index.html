Frequently asked questions

How does the AI symptom checker work?

You describe your symptoms in your own words — what you feel, how long, how severe, and any other symptoms. MediMind responds with clear general information on common possible causes and when to see a doctor. It is educational, not a diagnosis.

Is it free?

Yes — free in your browser or the Android app, any time, in any language.

Can it tell me exactly what I have?

No. It explains common possibilities and red flags, but cannot examine you or give a diagnosis. A clinician is needed to diagnose and treat.

When should I seek urgent care?

For chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke signs, severe bleeding, a severe allergic reaction, or thoughts of self-harm, seek emergency care immediately — do not wait for an app.
