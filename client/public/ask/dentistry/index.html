Frequently asked questions

How do I relieve a toothache?

MedDroid explains general relief and what a persistent or severe toothache (or facial swelling) means — the latter needs prompt dental care.

What causes bleeding gums?

Usually gum inflammation from plaque; MedDroid explains care and when it points to something needing a dentist.

When do I need urgent dental care?

Facial swelling, severe pain, a knocked-out tooth or trauma need urgent attention — MedDroid flags these.
