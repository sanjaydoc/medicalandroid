When to see a doctor urgently

  • Sudden “worst-ever” headache, or weakness/speech trouble — emergency
  • Leg weakness or bladder/bowel changes with back pain — urgent
  • Progressive numbness or weakness in limbs
  • A brain or spine lesion needing surgical opinion

Frequently asked questions

Does a slipped disc always need surgery?

No — most improve with time, physiotherapy and pain management. Surgery is considered for severe or worsening nerve compression, weakness, or loss of bladder/bowel control.

What symptoms need a neurosurgeon urgently?

A sudden severe headache, new weakness or speech difficulty, or back pain with leg weakness or bladder/bowel changes — these need emergency assessment.
