When to see a doctor urgently

  • Sudden vision loss or a “curtain” over vision — emergency
  • Severe eye pain with redness and nausea
  • Flashes with a shower of new floaters
  • An eye injury or chemical splash

Frequently asked questions

How often should diabetics have eye checks?

At least once a year — diabetes can silently damage the retina, and early treatment protects sight. Sudden vision change needs prompt review.

Is cataract surgery safe?

Cataract surgery is one of the most common and successful operations, usually restoring clear vision. Your ophthalmologist advises on timing.
