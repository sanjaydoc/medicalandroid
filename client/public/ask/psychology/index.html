Frequently asked questions

What’s the difference between a psychologist and a psychiatrist?

MediMind explains it simply: psychologists focus on talking therapy and counselling; psychiatrists are doctors who can also prescribe medicine. Many people benefit from both.

How can I manage stress and anxiety day to day?

MediMind shares general, evidence-based coping strategies (breathing, routine, sleep, activity) and when professional counselling would help.

When should I see a counsellor or therapist?

If low mood, stress or worry is affecting your daily life, relationships or sleep, talking to a professional helps — MediMind encourages it and explains what to expect.
