Vascular Surgery

Vascular surgeons treat blood-vessel conditions (other than the heart) — blocked arteries, varicose veins, aneurysms and clots.

When to see a doctor urgently

  • A painful, swollen, warm calf — urgent (possible DVT)
  • Leg pain on walking that eases with rest (claudication)
  • A non-healing foot ulcer, especially with diabetes
  • Sudden cold, pale, painful limb — emergency

Frequently asked questions

What is peripheral artery disease?

Narrowed leg arteries from fatty build-up, causing pain on walking and, if severe, non-healing wounds. It also signals higher heart and stroke risk, so it is managed seriously.

Are varicose veins dangerous?

Usually they are a cosmetic and comfort issue, but they can cause skin changes or ulcers over time. A vascular surgeon advises when treatment helps.
