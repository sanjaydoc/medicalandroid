Hair Loss (PRP)

Written & medically reviewed by Dr. Sanjay Anbu, MBBS · Last reviewed 22 September 2026

Hair loss has many causes, the most common being genetic (pattern) baldness. PRP (platelet-rich plasma) injections are marketed for hair loss, but the evidence is limited and results vary.

Causes & risk factors

  • Genetic (male- or female-pattern) hair loss
  • Stress, illness or after childbirth (temporary shedding)
  • Thyroid problems or iron deficiency
  • Some medicines and treatments
  • Scalp conditions
  • Tight hairstyles or hair treatments

Common symptoms

  • Gradual thinning, often at the crown or hairline
  • A receding hairline (men)
  • Widening parting (women)
  • Sudden shedding after stress or illness
  • Patchy bald spots (may point to another cause)

Tests & diagnosis

  • Examination of the scalp and hair
  • Iron studies
  • Thyroid tests
  • Scalp biopsy in some cases

Treatment & management

  • Treat any underlying cause (iron, thyroid, medicines)
  • Proven medical treatments for pattern hair loss
  • Hair transplant surgery for suitable cases
  • PRP is investigational for hair loss — evidence is limited and effects modest
  • Be wary of clinics promising dramatic regrowth

When to see a doctor urgently

  • Sudden, patchy or rapid hair loss
  • Hair loss with scalp pain, redness or scarring
  • Before paying for PRP — check proven options first

Frequently asked questions

Does PRP regrow hair?

Evidence is limited. Some small studies suggest modest improvement in thickness for pattern hair loss, but results vary and it is not a guaranteed or standard treatment. Proven medical options should be considered first.

Can treating iron or thyroid help my hair?

Yes, if those are the cause. Low iron and thyroid problems can cause hair thinning, and correcting them can help — which is why a check-up is worthwhile before pursuing costly treatments.
