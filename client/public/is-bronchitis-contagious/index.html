Is bronchitis contagious?

Written & medically reviewed by Dr. Sanjay Anbu, MBBS · Last reviewed 26 September 2026

It depends on the type. Acute bronchitis is usually caused by a virus and can spread like a cold; long-term (chronic) bronchitis from smoking or COPD is not contagious. Here is what to know.

The short answer

  • Acute bronchitis (viral): yes, contagious like a cold or flu
  • It spreads through coughs, sneezes and contaminated hands/surfaces
  • Most contagious in the first few days, often while you have a fever
  • Chronic bronchitis (smoking/COPD): not contagious
  • Cover coughs, wash hands and rest to avoid spreading it

When to see a doctor urgently

  • Breathlessness, wheezing or chest pain
  • A cough lasting more than three weeks or bringing up blood
  • High fever, or symptoms in a baby, elderly or frail person
Antibiotics rarely help. Most acute bronchitis is viral, so antibiotics usually do not help. MedDroid gives general information, not a diagnosis.

Frequently asked questions

How long is bronchitis contagious?

For viral acute bronchitis, you are usually most contagious in the first few days and while feverish. The cough itself can linger for weeks after you are no longer infectious.

Is chronic bronchitis contagious?

No. Chronic bronchitis is caused by long-term irritation, usually from smoking or COPD, and does not spread from person to person.

Do I need antibiotics for bronchitis?

Usually not — most cases are viral. Antibiotics are only useful if a doctor finds a bacterial infection. Rest, fluids and time are the mainstays.
