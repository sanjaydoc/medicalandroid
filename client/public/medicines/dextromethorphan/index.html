Dextromethorphan (cough): uses, side effects & brands

Written & medically reviewed by Dr. Sanjay Anbu, MBBS · Last reviewed 26 September 2026

Dextromethorphan is a cough suppressant for a dry, tickly cough. It belongs to the Respiratory / cough & cold medicine group. These ease breathing, loosen mucus or calm a cough in chest and airway conditions.

What it is used for

  • Asthma and COPD (inhalers)
  • Loosening or calming a cough
  • Wheeze and breathlessness
  • Blocked airways

Common brand names

  • India: Corex DX, Benadryl DR
  • Worldwide: Robitussin DM, Delsym

Common side effects

  • Shakiness or fast heartbeat (relievers)
  • Hoarseness or oral thrush (steroid inhalers)
  • Drowsiness (some cough medicines)

Before taking it — precautions

  • Rinse the mouth after steroid inhalers
  • Use a spacer with inhalers for better delivery
  • Do not rely on cough syrup for a lasting cough — get it checked

When to see a doctor urgently

  • Severe breathlessness, blue lips or unable to speak (emergency)
  • Reliever inhaler not working
  • A cough lasting more than 3 weeks or with blood

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a reliever and a preventer inhaler?

A reliever opens the airways quickly during symptoms; a preventer (often a steroid) is taken daily to reduce inflammation and prevent attacks.

Do cough syrups actually work?

Evidence is limited. Fluids, steam and treating the cause help most. A lasting or severe cough should be checked rather than just suppressed.
