Erectile dysfunction: causes and help

Written & medically reviewed by Dr. Sanjay Anbu, MBBS · Last reviewed 26 September 2026

Erectile dysfunction is common and very treatable, and it is nothing to be ashamed of. Importantly, it can be an early warning sign of heart or blood-vessel problems — so it is worth understanding, not just ignoring.

Common causes & what helps

  • Physical: diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, smoking, obesity
  • Psychological: stress, anxiety, depression, relationship worries
  • Lifestyle: exercise, weight loss, stopping smoking and limiting alcohol help a lot
  • Medicines like sildenafil (Viagra) or tadalafil (Cialis) are effective and safe for most men
  • Treating the underlying cause (e.g. diabetes, low testosterone) often improves it

When to see a doctor urgently

  • Never combine ED tablets with nitrate heart medicines (dangerous drop in blood pressure)
  • New ED can be an early sign of heart/blood-vessel disease — get a health check
  • An erection lasting over 4 hours (priapism) is an emergency
  • Sudden ED with numbness or difficulty passing urine — see a doctor
It is common, treatable — and a health signal. ED shares causes with heart disease, so it is worth a check of blood pressure, sugar and cholesterol. Do not buy unregulated “sex tablets”. MedDroid gives general information, not a diagnosis.

Frequently asked questions

What causes erectile dysfunction?

Often a mix of physical (diabetes, blood-pressure, cholesterol, smoking) and psychological (stress, anxiety) causes. Because it involves blood flow, it can also flag early heart disease.

Do ED tablets like Viagra work?

Yes — sildenafil and tadalafil work for most men and are safe when prescribed. They must never be combined with nitrate heart medicines. Avoid unregulated online “sex pills”.

Can ED be cured naturally?

Lifestyle changes — exercise, weight loss, stopping smoking, less alcohol, and treating diabetes/BP — genuinely improve erections, sometimes enough on their own.
