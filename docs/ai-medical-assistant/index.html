Frequently asked questions

What is an AI medical assistant?

A chatbot that answers health questions in plain language — explaining symptoms, medicines, lab reports and scans. MediMind is free and works in any language, but it gives general information, not a diagnosis.

Can I choose a speciality?

Yes — focus MediMind on cardiology, dermatology, paediatrics, orthopaedics, neurology and more, so the answer is oriented to that area.

Does it replace my doctor?

No. It helps you understand your health and prepare for appointments, and always points you to a clinician for diagnosis and treatment.

Is there an app?

Yes — MediMind works in your browser and as a free Android app.
