When to see a doctor urgently

  • Severe sudden abdominal pain with a rigid tummy — emergency
  • A painful, irreducible or tender lump/hernia
  • A lump that is growing or hard
  • Right-lower abdominal pain with fever (appendicitis)

Frequently asked questions

What conditions need general surgery?

Common ones include appendicitis, hernias, gallstones and piles, plus removal of lumps. Many are now done by keyhole (laparoscopic) surgery.

What is keyhole surgery?

Laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery uses small cuts and a camera, usually meaning less pain and faster recovery than open surgery for suitable operations.
