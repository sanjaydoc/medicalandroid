When to see a doctor urgently

  • High fever with confusion, a stiff neck or a non-fading rash — emergency
  • Fever lasting more than 5–7 days
  • Fever after travel to a malaria/dengue region
  • Persistent night sweats and weight loss

Frequently asked questions

Do I always need antibiotics for an infection?

No — many infections are viral and do not respond to antibiotics. A specialist helps decide when they are truly needed to avoid resistance.

What tests diagnose typhoid, dengue and malaria?

Blood culture (best) or Widal for typhoid; NS1/IgM for dengue; blood smear or rapid test for malaria — chosen by the day of illness and symptoms.
