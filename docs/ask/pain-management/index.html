When to see a doctor urgently

  • Pain lasting more than 3 months despite treatment
  • Nerve pain, numbness or weakness
  • Pain limiting sleep, work or daily life
  • Back pain with red flags — see a doctor urgently

Frequently asked questions

What does a pain-management specialist do?

They treat long-standing or complex pain using a mix of medicines, targeted injections, physiotherapy and other techniques to improve function and quality of life.

Are pain injections safe?

Procedures like nerve blocks and epidural injections are commonly used and generally safe when done by a specialist. They explain the benefits and risks for your case.
