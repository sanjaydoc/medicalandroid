Frequently asked questions

How do I read my blood test results?

Each line shows a test, your value, the unit, and the lab's reference (normal) range. A value inside the range is typical; outside it is flagged high or low. MedDroid reads your report and explains each line in plain language — but only your doctor can interpret it against your full history.

What does it mean if a blood value is high or low?

A value outside the reference range can have many causes — some harmless (recent food, exercise, hydration, the lab's method) and some needing follow-up. MedDroid explains the common reasons a marker moves and which results usually warrant a doctor's review.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. It is general educational information to help you understand your report and ask better questions. It does not replace your doctor or lab.

Which blood tests can MedDroid explain?

Common panels including CBC, liver function, kidney function, lipid profile, blood sugar (including HbA1c), thyroid, and vitamins such as D and B12.
