Frequently asked questions

Can MediMind tell me if two medicines interact?

It explains common, well-known interactions in general terms and flags when to check with a pharmacist. For your exact medicines and doses, always confirm with your doctor or pharmacist.

Is it safe to take painkillers together?

Some combinations are fine and some are risky (and many products already contain the same drug). MediMind explains the general rules and when to be careful.

What should I do about a side effect?

MediMind explains which side effects are usually mild and which need prompt medical attention — and always says to seek care for severe reactions.
