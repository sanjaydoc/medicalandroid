Frequently asked questions

Can AI interpret my MRI?

MedDroid explains what an MRI or its report appears to show in simple words, but it is educational only — a radiologist and your treating doctor must confirm.

What do "hyperintensity" and similar terms mean?

MedDroid translates common MRI terms into plain language and explains, in general, what they can indicate — without replacing the specialist read.

Is an incidental finding dangerous?

Many incidental findings are harmless, but some need follow-up. MedDroid explains the general picture and urges you to confirm with your doctor.
