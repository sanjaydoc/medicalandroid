How to get glowing skin

Written & medically reviewed by Dr. Sanjay Anbu, MBBS · Last reviewed 26 September 2026

“Glowing skin” really means healthy, well-cared-for skin — and it comes from a simple, consistent routine plus good habits, not expensive miracle products or fairness creams.

A simple routine that works

  • Cleanse gently, moisturise, and use sunscreen every morning
  • Add a treatment (vitamin C in the morning, a retinoid at night) if you wish
  • Stay hydrated, sleep well, and eat plenty of fruit and vegetables
  • Do not over-exfoliate or pile on too many products
  • Be consistent — results take weeks, not days

When to see a doctor urgently

  • Skin problems (acne, rashes, pigmentation) need targeted treatment
  • Avoid unregulated fairness/bleaching products
  • Reactions to new products — patch test and simplify
Healthy beats “fair”. A simple routine, sun protection and good habits give healthy, glowing skin. Skip fairness creams and “detox” gimmicks. MedDroid gives general information, not a diagnosis.

Frequently asked questions

How can I get glowing skin naturally?

Cleanse gently, moisturise, use sunscreen daily, stay hydrated, sleep well and eat plenty of fruit and vegetables. Consistency matters more than expensive products.

What is the best skincare routine?

A simple one: cleanser, moisturiser and sunscreen by day; cleanser, moisturiser and (optionally) a retinoid at night. Add vitamin C if you like. Do not overdo products.

Do diet and water affect skin?

Yes — a balanced diet, enough water and good sleep support healthy skin, though no single food gives an instant “glow”.
